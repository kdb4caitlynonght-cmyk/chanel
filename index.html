Celebrating the CHANEL codes, the Spring-Summer 2025 Ready-to-Wear collection is an ode to delicacy, lightness and movement. Aviator jackets with Peter Pan collars, flight suits and uniform dresses make way for a confident allure in tribute to pioneering female figures, such as the first airwomen or the legendary French author Colette who, like Gabrielle Chanel, flouted the conventions of their time.
